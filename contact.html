What is TeamFlow?

TeamFlow is a group task management platform that helps teams create and assign work, track progress, review submissions, and recognize contributions in one shared workspace.

How does skill matching help my team?

Members can share their skills and proficiency levels, while task requirements help leaders identify suitable people for each piece of work.

How are tasks reviewed and contributions tracked?

Members submit completed work for review. Leaders can provide feedback, request changes, approve deliverables, and use visible progress and contribution metrics to understand team effort.
